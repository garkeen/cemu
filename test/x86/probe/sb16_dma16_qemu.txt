Lock cell for the same reason as sb16_dma8: QEMU's IsaDma::read_memory path
leaves the 8257's count/status alone (tc=00 cnt=003f), while cemu walks the
board's 8237 through DRQ/DACK and the controller spends its word count and
latches channel 5's TC (tc=02 cnt=ffff). The SB16-visible fields, the IRQ16
latch and its 0x22f acknowledge, agree.

  sb16-dma16 irq=02 tc=02 cnt=ffff ack=00 ok

irq   mixer 0x82 after the block: bit 1, the 16-bit latch
tc    8237 slave status register: channel 5's terminal-count bit
cnt   channel 5 word count after the block: spent (0xffff)
ack   mixer 0x82 after reading 0x22f: the latch cleared

The guest fills its 128-byte buffer with zero, signed 16-bit silence, and the
host PCM output is off by default, so neither run makes sound.
