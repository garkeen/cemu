      -display none -no-reboot -serial stdio              -> pm-sci FAIL step=2

Where they agree: PMBASE at 00:01.3 offset 0x40 and PMREGMISC 0x80 decode the
32-byte block, PM1_STS/EN/CNT reset, PM1_STS W1C, PM1_EN, SCI_EN, the SCI on
IRQ9 and S5 power-off. QEMU's PM timer also starts with TMR_STS set; the probe
clears the fixed-event bits before checking the reset state.

The divergence is GBL_RLS: ACPI 4.8 makes PM1_CNT bit 2 a write-only release
action that sets GBL_STS (bit 5) and reads back 0. cemu implements that; QEMU
stores the bit and leaves GBL_STS clear, so the probe's GBL event check fails
there. The probe is a lock cell: cemu is pinned to the manual's behavior, and
the QEMU divergence is recorded here rather than silently accepted.
