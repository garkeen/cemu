V86 hardware-interrupt nesting (test/x86/probe/v86_irq.asm): three IRQs land
while the previous reflected V86 handler is still live, so the saved ESP walks
0x7000 -> 0x6ffa -> 0x6ff4 (the shape of the DOS/EMM386 crash).

The block below is cemu's transcript, which the cell pins. QEMU prints the
same F21/mark/irqtab lines and the same ESP/SS/CS nesting, but two fields are
arrival-time artifacts rather than semantics, and differ by design:
    IRQ0 fl: cemu 0x23216, QEMU 0x23293   (which instruction the IRQ hit)
    cnt:     cemu 0x10000, QEMU 0x38f10   (the probe's instruction-axis tick)
The levels 1/2 flags (0x23293/0x23297) agree between the models, and both exit
with status 11 (captured 2026-10-30).
