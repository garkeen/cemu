The block below is assembled by the cell from the probe's marker lines and is
byte-identical under cemu and
  qemu-system-i386 -machine pc -m 32 -kernel v86_int.elf -display none \
      -no-reboot -serial file:v86_int_qemu.txt \
      -device isa-debug-exit,iobase=0xf4,iosize=0x4
(both exit payload 5 -> status 11; captured 2026-10-30). F20 is a 32-bit DPL3
interrupt gate entered from V86: the saved SS:ESP is the V86 stack and
ES/DS/FS/GS are cleared in the handler. F22 is the nested INT 0x22 while the
outer 3-word frame is live (saved ESP 0x6ffa). GP/REP are the I/O-bitmap #GP
on OUT and on REP OUTSW -- the architectural state EMM386's reflector reads to
resume the transfer.
