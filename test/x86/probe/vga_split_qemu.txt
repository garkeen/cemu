Dual run of test/x86/probe/vga_split.asm (vga_paint's 640x350 planar mode with
R18=175, R9 bit 6 clear, AR13=2), 2026-10-21:

  cemu --machine x86 --isa x86 vga_split.elf             -> vga-split ok
  qemu-system-i386 -machine pc -m 32 -kernel vga_split.elf -vga std \
      -display none -no-reboot -serial file:...          -> vga-split ok

Both models accept the split and panning registers and pass the probe. The
picture is judged in cemu by fbdump: the full white row painted at VRAM row
100 appears at displayed row 276 (175 + 101), the blue row 300 does not, and
every row is shifted by the two pel-panning pixels. QEMU 11's screendump with
-display none/vnc reports its 640x480 console surface for this probe (also for
the unmodified vga_paint), so the picture comparison is cemu's fbdump against
the unmodified vga_paint frame plus the register semantics QEMU shares.
