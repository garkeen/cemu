Lock cell: there is no reference implementation to match. QEMU's
hw/timer/i8254.c stores the control word's BCD bit (s->bcd) but the counter
stays binary; its status-latch comment still says "XXX: add BCD and null
count". The probe therefore fails on QEMU at the first sample that would be a
non-decimal nibble (0x0f), which is the line below.

  pit-bcd ok

What the probe pins (Intel 8254 datasheet, control word bit 0):

  hi     load 0x1234 in BCD mode and latch immediately. The high byte is 0x12;
         a binary countdown of 1234 decimal reads 0x04 (0x04d2).
  tens   re-program with 8-bit access and BCD 0x0016, then sample the low byte
         200000 times. Every sample must have both nibbles <= 9, and at least
         one sample must be 0x11..0x16. A binary counter walks through
         0x0a..0x0f, which are not BCD digits.
